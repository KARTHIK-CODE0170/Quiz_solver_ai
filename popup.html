📖 How to Use
⚠️ This extension does NOT work with Google Gemini. Please use ChatGPT, Copilot, or Claude instead.
  1. Open your course — Navigate to the Coursera or Aditya Maya assessment page in your browser.
  2. Choose your AI — In the popup, select your preferred AI from the dropdown (ChatGPT, Copilot, or Claude).
  3. Log into the AI — Make sure you're logged into the selected AI service in another tab.
  4. Click "Solve Current Page" — This solves only the quiz currently visible on the page.
  5. Or click "Auto-Solve All Tasks" — This runs the full pipeline and solves all quizzes automatically.
  6. Wait for completion — The extension will pick answers and submit them. Don't close the browser.
  7. Check results — Once done, review your submitted answers on the course page.
💡 Tip: Enable "Run silently in background" to let the solver work without switching tabs.
